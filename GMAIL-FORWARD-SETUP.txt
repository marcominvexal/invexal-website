Steps (5 minutes):
------------------
1. Open https://mail.google.com  (login: marcominvexal@gmail.com)

2. Click the gear icon (top right) → "See all settings"

3. Tab: "Forwarding and POP/IMAP"

4. Click "Add a forwarding address"
   Enter: danish.khan@invexal.com
   Click Next → Proceed → OK

5. Open danish.khan@invexal.com inbox (or ask Danish to check)
   Click the verification link from Google

6. Back in Gmail settings → Forwarding:
   Select: "Forward a copy of incoming mail to danish.khan@invexal.com"
   Choose: "keep Gmail's copy in the Inbox" (recommended)
   Click Save Changes

Done. All future Gmail emails (including website forms) copy to Danish.
